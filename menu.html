Meeting History — Pick your next encounter

You have tried Socrates. He questions you, so you learn to ask questions. From here, you do the questioning, and the dead answer. Each figure speaks only from its own writings and record. Where the corpus is silent, the figure is silent. You judge.

Pick a figure by subject. Its page copies the encounter prompt and opens ChatGPT. Paste it as your first message: Windows Control+V, Mac Command+V.
