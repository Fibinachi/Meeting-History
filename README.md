# Meeting History

A library of encounters with individual historical figures, run through a conversational AI. The student meets one person — Socrates, or whoever comes next — talks with them by asking questions, and leaves having had to think. The first entry, *Meet Socrates*, is in development.

## The point

Every encounter ends with the student judging who this person was. Not reciting facts about them — judging them: morally complex, intellectually complex, an actual person rather than a legend or a myth. The corpus rule below is what makes that possible. Legends are what accrete after the writing stops; staying inside the written work keeps them human.

## The dead rule

They have to be dead. It's called Meeting *History* — and you can't judge a life that isn't finished. The corpus is closed, the record is complete, and the student is judging a whole person, not a work in progress. No living figures, no exceptions.

## The corpus rule

Every figure in this library speaks from the corpus of their written body of work — their own writings, their recorded words, the primary sources that carry their voice. The figure may claim only what the corpus supports. Where the corpus is silent, the figure is silent or ignorant in period-appropriate ways, never inventive. This is the whole discipline of the library: the voice is bounded by the texts, and the texts are listed with every figure.

## Curated, not crowdsourced

Anyone may suggest a figure. Only 14QBD273 authors them. Use the [suggestion template](.github/ISSUE_TEMPLATE/suggest-a-figure.md) to propose who should be met next, why meeting them matters, and what primary sources you know of. A suggestion grants no authorship and no promise — curation is the product, and it stays in-house.

## The scaffold

Every figure is built on shared machinery in [`scaffold/`](scaffold/):

- [tutorial-template.md](scaffold/tutorial-template.md) — the modern out-of-character guide that opens each encounter and teaches the student the mechanics.
- [engagement-machinery.md](scaffold/engagement-machinery.md) — the interaction rules: short answers, hooks, voices that ask back, bracketed suggestions, stall recovery, observational nudges.
- [debrief-template.md](scaffold/debrief-template.md) — the fixed-order closing, out of character.
- [voice-construction-guide.md](scaffold/voice-construction-guide.md) — how to author a figure's voice from its corpus. Read this first.
- [age-gate-template.md](scaffold/age-gate-template.md) — proposed student-version design (not yet approved).
- [standards-template.md](scaffold/standards-template.md) — how to align each figure to C3, Common Core, and state standards.

New figures start from [`figures/_template/`](figures/_template/), a blank folder with a prompt skeleton, a sources template, and teacher instructions.
